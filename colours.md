## Design Principles

1. **Burnt Orange is the action colour** -- it draws attention to CTAs, active states, and key metrics.
2. **Dark Navy Blue provides authority** -- used for page hero banners and the footer to anchor the layout.
3. **White and Light Grey alternate** to create visual rhythm between content sections.
4. **WhatsApp Green** is preserved at its brand value for trust and instant recognition.
5. **Shadows use the orange hue** on interactive elements so they feel cohesive rather than generic.
6. **Text hierarchy** uses Near Black for headings and Muted Indigo-Grey for secondary text, with enough contrast to meet accessibility standards.
